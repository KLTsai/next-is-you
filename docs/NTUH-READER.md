# NTUH first-hospital reader (Issue #20)

Supported scope: NTU Hospital main campus (branch `T0`), department ophthalmology (`OPH`), morning, afternoon or evening session of the day the organizer entered, read from the official public registration progress list on `registration.ntuh.gov.tw`. Other departments, other dates, and Shin Kong stay explicitly unavailable. The session is the `AmpmCode` the reader sends, not a clock: no session needs verified hours to be read. The published start times (Reading rules, "Match" and "Cadence") decide when the day's scheduled reads begin and when a missing card counts as an unusable read. An evening session is read but today sends no progress reminder, because no one has observed one (Reading rules, "Unobserved situations"), and a source that cannot be read still sends the unavailable message; #183 makes it follow the same rules as any other session and offers it only where its page was captured (captain, 2026-11-22). Evidence: Issue #17 report, plus the 2026-10-29 reads that captured the request contract, the fragment's markup and how a clinic starts and ends; the owner approved scheduled reads for family-internal, non-commercial use at one read every 2 to 3 minutes.

**Validation status: fixture and local integration only. Live hospital behavior is unverified.** No test or CI run contacts the hospital. Source evidence is not LINE acceptance or handset delivery.

## Enabling

The reader is off by default. `NTUH_READER_ENABLED=1` enables it only in local-test/rehearsal mode. No deployed environment reads the live hospital or activates reminders until it is live (`docs/PRE-LIVE-CHECKLIST.md`); Start reports the source unavailable, scheduled ticks remain held, and scheduler status reports the reader unavailable. A trustworthy observation means the fetched source read is fresh, matches the configured supported scope and has one unambiguous positive current number. The source list does not label department or session, so the requested department and session are not confirmed by the returned row. Trust does not mean the number is accurate against the room's physical light; that accuracy is unverified. Live behavior remains unverified.

## Entering the clinic

The reminder form has no doctor or room fields, so the NTUH clinic code carries them as `room/doctor/clinic type`, for example `11診/王小明/醫師約診`, with department code `OPH`. On the visit day the reader identifies the clinic's card by room and doctor (compared ignoring case, spaces and punctuation, and with the room's 診 suffix optional, so `03` and `03 診` are the same room); see Reading rules for the clinic type and substitute doctors. The reader pins the source's Traditional Chinese rendering, because that is what the owner's registration slip says; the same page in English renders the same clinic as `Room 11 / Hsiao-Ming Wang / Dr. Appt.`, which would not match. Any other shape is rejected as a scope mismatch without a request.

## How a read is made

One read is one page: the session's list, or, once the clinic's card is found, that clinic's detail page (one GET; Reading rules, "Detail page"). A page that fails may fall back to the other in the same read ("A link that fails").

The site's own page does not put clinic rows in the progress page's HTML. One read of the list is two requests:

1. `GET https://registration.ntuh.gov.tw/WebReg/WebReg/ClinicCurrentLightNo?vHospCode=T0` for the `__RequestVerificationToken` hidden input and the session cookies. No clinic rows come back here. The page renders 3 different token values, all changing on every load; the reader sends the first (a language-switch form's), which every list request on 2026-11-25 accepted.
2. `POST https://registration.ntuh.gov.tw/WebReg/WebReg/DeptLightTable` with those cookies, `X-Requested-With: XMLHttpRequest`, and the form body `__RequestVerificationToken`, `vHospitalCode=T0`, `DeptCode`, `RegionCode=`, and `AmpmCode` (`1` morning, `2` afternoon, `3` evening). The reply is an HTML fragment of clinic cards.

Both requests send `Accept-Language: zh-TW` and the `Localization.CurrentUICulture=zh-TW` cookie, so the rendering stays Traditional Chinese whatever the server would otherwise negotiate. The pair shares one 10-second budget and never follows redirects. Because the department and the session are now request parameters, the returned cards are the ones for the department and session the owner entered, rather than whatever a default view would have returned.

A card looks like this, and the reader reads `room-number`, `clinic-doc-name`, `clinic-type`, `number`, `according-to-order` and the link's `ServiceIDSE` from it:

```html
<a href="/WebReg/WebReg/ClinicCurrentLightNoDetail?ServiceIDSE=..." class="open-link">
  <div class="room-number">11 診</div>
  <div class="row room-content g-0">
    <div class="col-7">
      <div class="clinic-doc-name">王小明</div>
      <div class="clinic-type">醫師約診</div>
    </div>
    <div class="col-5 align-items-stretch">
      <div class="number"> 025 </div>
      <div class="according-to-order pt-2"></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</a>
```

## Reading rules

- Match (Issue #135): the visit's card is identified in the session's list by room and doctor, with the room's 診 suffix optional. The clinic type is not compared across the schedule and the card, because the schedule's 診別 and the card's clinic type are different vocabularies; a card that also agrees on the clinic type wins, which tells apart two cards of one doctor when the visit stores the card's own type. With the booked doctor absent, one `(代)` card in the same room is the visit's card and the visit is marked `substitute_doctor` (it keeps running, and the organizer decides). Before the clinic has called, a missing card is `card_not_found`. Before the session's published start (09:00 morning, 13:30 afternoon, 18:00 evening as a stand-in, Taipei) it is stored for the organizer but is not an unusable read, so it never reaches a family's unavailable message; from the published start on, it counts toward that message. After the clinic has called, a card missing from a list read means the clinic ended. Several cards are `ambiguous_match` and never produce a number. Identification runs on every read of the list. NTUH lists the day's cards from about 00:40, but the first scheduled read is one hour before the published start ("Cadence"), so that is when the organizer first learns that a card is missing or that a substitute has it, unless Start on the visit day found it earlier (captain, 2026-11-22, #181). It runs again every 30 minutes until the clinic calls (captain, 2026-11-22): a card that disappears, or a substitute doctor's card that replaces it, is noticed within that time. After the clinic calls, the list is read again only when the detail page stops being the clinic's, so a change of doctor in mid-session is not looked for. The current number is never inferred from neighbors, and the fragment's "Last updated" and the detail page's own render time are ignored.
- Detail page (Issue #163): the read that finds the card keeps its `ServiceIDSE` link for that day, and the reads after it are that clinic's detail page (one GET, no token or session cookie, only the pinned language, its own 10-second budget). The list is read while the card is not found, so `card_not_found` can still clear when the card appears, and for the 30-minute identification above. If that check fails, the detail page is read in the same read and the list is asked again after 5 minutes. A source that asks to be left alone (429, or any `Retry-After`) is left alone on both pages, and a refusal stops the source for the day as any refusal does. The page's current light feeds the existing current-number fields, so the near and unavailable behaviour is unchanged; the flag under the light, 預計叫號, the status of every number still on the list and, only when proven ("Highest called" below), its 已叫最大號 ride on the observation. A list read states no number statuses, so they are absent from it (`numbers` is `None`), never an empty list: an empty list is a detail page with no number left.
- Highest called (#248, ADR 0003): one rule for every department, with no table of departments. The observation reports the page's 已叫最大號 as highest called only when the page proves that number was called: it is listed as 看診中, or it is no longer on the page's list of numbers. A number listed only as 未報到, 初診 or 已報到 is not proven: the observation then carries no highest called, only the 預計叫號 numbers the page states. The check is `proven_highest_called` in `domain.py`, which any hospital's reader can call. The reader's own checks (called in this session, the stale link and a later block) still read the page's raw 已叫最大號.
- A link that fails: a detail page is not the clinic's when it answers an unexpected status (404, 410 or another that is neither a refusal nor a passing failure), has no usable progress panel, or names another room, or when it has not moved since before the clinic called while the list card shows another number. A page that fails twice in a row (5xx or a timeout) is treated the same, because a dead link may answer 500. Its number is never used. The link is dropped and the list is read in the same read, so the visit goes on as it would from the list alone; the same link is not tried again for 30 minutes. Doctors are not compared, because a substitute's card names no doctor. One 5xx or timeout is an ordinary failed read and keeps the link. A redirect stays a refusal that stops reading the department and session for the day: no one has observed what a dead link answers. A card with no detail link is read from the list alone; none of the 2,608 cards captured on 2026-11-20 lacked one.
- Called in this session (Issue #163): a detail page is not cleared after its session, so a number on it is not proof the clinic has called. The first page read before the clinic called is kept as the frozen state. The observation's `called_in_session` is true once the list card showed a number, or once the page shows a light, a highest called or a check-in position that the frozen state did not; a value that only disappeared is not a call. When the page shows a current light that no read saw change, the list is asked once, at the next read, because its card is the session's own. The reader does not ask the list about a check-in-order clinic, so there an unchanged position leaves `called_in_session` unknown, although such a card does show the number being seen ("Observed across five departments" below). Until the clinic has called, the visit is pending: not a failed read, nothing sent.
- Facts without a current number (Issue #163): a page that was read but has no current number to report (pending, blank after a call, check-in order, or an unobserved situation) is a skipped read that still carries the page's facts (`ReadSkipped.progress`, never trusted as a current number) for the rules that use them. It ends a run of unusable reads and clears the number shown to the organizer.
- Check-in order: the reader gives a clinic whose card or page says it calls by check-in order (依報到順序看診) no called number. That rule assumed its light is not a patient number; on 2026-11-25 its card and light showed the patient number being seen ("Observed across five departments" below). It is `clinic_not_started` until its page is seen to move and `check_in_order` after, and the facts of its detail page hold its 報到順序 and every number's position. List reads never end it. Nothing is sent until #167.
- Blank after a call: the light goes blank for good when a clinic ends (6 of 13 pages on 2026-11-20, and none resumed). The read is `blank_after_call`, shown as "來源沒有可用資料", is not an unusable read and sends nothing. #138 owns ending the visit. The list-card end rule below applies only to list reads, so until then a visit reads until the 23:59 cutoff.
- Unobserved situations (Issue #163, ADR 0003): an evening session, a highest called that falls below the highest already seen this session (a later block opening on the same page), and a clinic that calls again after a blank (a midday blank) have not been observed on NTUH's pages. Each is recorded once per clinic and day in the clinic's transient state and as an `ntuh_unobserved_situation` line in the Worker log (situation, session and date; no clinic, doctor or number). An evening session and a later block are `unobserved_situation`: the source status becomes "尚無院方觀察" (or the substitute doctor's) and nothing is sent. A midday blank sends nothing while it lasts; the numbers called after it are ordinary reads. A highest called left over from the frozen state is not this session's, so the page resetting when a session starts is not a later block.
- Blank current light means no data, never zero. `0`, non-numeric text or more than 6 digits is invalid data, on the list card and on the detail page.
- Freshness is the time the reader fetched the page. An observation older than 2 minutes cannot start a reminder or support a scheduled alert. A usable read is not evidence of physical-light accuracy; no accuracy claim is made.
- Watch window: reads happen on the day the organizer entered and nowhere else; the scheduler's 23:59 Taipei cutoff closes the visit from above. Within that day scheduled reads begin one hour before the published start ("Cadence, when reads begin"); from then on the clinic's own number marks the window. A clinic that has not called in this session has not started, which is not a failed read and sends no alert. The first valid number opens the watch. On list reads, the number going blank - or the card vanishing - for two reads in a row then ends it, and the clinic is not read again that day; a detail page never ends it.
- Cadence, when reads begin (captain, 2026-11-22, #181): scheduled reads of a visit's clinic begin one hour before its session's published start, at 08:00 morning, 12:30 afternoon and 17:00 evening (18:00 is the stand-in start). Before that the visit is pending: no request is made, no failure is counted, nothing is sent, and the organizer keeps seeing what Start found. Start on the visit day still reads, whatever the hour, because it must confirm the clinic; no scheduled read follows it until the reads begin, and the interval after a read still applies then. A clinic that called before its first read is seen as called, because its list card shows a number. The published start says when a session opens, not when a clinic first calls: on 2026-11-20 an afternoon clinic first showed a number between 16:05 and 16:15. So a clinic that has not called is read until 23:59, when its visit ends; one that called is read until its end (#138). A rule that stopped reading a clinic that had not called four hours after its published start was decided and withdrawn the same day (captain, 2026-11-22): it could silently miss a clinic that starts late in its session.
- Cadence, how often: the one-minute alarm only offers a chance to read. Each clinic has independent cadence and retries: while the card has no detail link yet (or has none at all), one list read every 5 minutes before the clinic has called anyone and one every 2 minutes from the read that first sees a number; once the link is found, one read every 2 minutes, before and after the clinic calls, of which the 30-minute list check takes the place of one, counted with every other live environment under the source access rules (`docs/ADDING-A-HOSPITAL.md`). Repeated reads for the same clinic in one tick share its result; different clinics read independently. Transient Durable Object state retains only the matched card for that clinic (room, doctor, clinic type, current number and link) and, once found, the fields parsed from its detail page (never the raw response), and what the rules above need: the link in use and the one that failed last, whether the clinic has called and the card is a substitute's, the frozen state and the card's last number, the highest called and any blank seen, the unobserved situations recorded, when each page is next due, and the current run of blank or failed reads; unrelated cards and raw responses are discarded. State of any other day, the detail-page link included, is dropped at the start of every scheduler tick, so within 5 minutes of midnight Taipei, whether or not sending or reading is held or the reader is off. Cross-clinic read sharing may be considered as a future optimization outside Issue #20.
- Failures: a timeout, HTTP 408/429/5xx or other transport error retries after 1, then 2, then 4 minutes (capped), honoring `Retry-After` up to 10 minutes. A challenge or CAPTCHA marker, HTTP 401/403/451, or any redirect on any request stops reading that source for the day and shows "來源拒絕讀取". A selector page without a token fails closed as `schema_drift` and the POST is never sent. Nothing bypasses, solves or works around a challenge; the only cookies sent back are the ones that read's own selector request received, and none are kept between reads.
- Unusable reads inside the watch feed the existing unavailable alert (third consecutive unusable read, once per recipient). Skipped ticks, a clinic that has not started, a page with no current number to report and a clinic that has ended are not unusable reads; a page that was read ends the run. A clinic ending can never raise that alert on its own: two blank reads end the watch and three are needed to alert.
- End of clinic, on list reads only: the first blank read after the clinic has been calling is an unusable read, because a single blank may be the source hiccuping. The second one ends the watch: reading stops, no unavailable alert is sent, and the reminder shows "來源沒有可用資料" until it expires at the normal cutoff.
- If the reader's transient state is lost mid-clinic, the clinic reads as not yet started again. That keeps the watch open rather than declaring an end that was never observed.

## Source evidence and known gaps

The request contract and the card markup above were captured on 2026-10-29 with two plain requests against the owner's host, and the parser reads that captured page correctly (`research/private/source-evidence/ntuh-oph-clinic-end-2026-10-29.md`). What remains unverified is the reader's own behavior against the live source: no test, CI run or deployment has issued these requests from the Worker. Replay tests read real selector, list, detail and clinic schedule pages captured on 2026-11-20, 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-25 (`tests/fixtures/hospitals/ntuh_captures/`, ADR 0006, #205), each committed as served with one leading comment naming its capture; the anti-forgery token values in the selector and schedule pages are replaced by a fixed placeholder. A schedule page over the reader's size cap (內科 and 外科部 on 2026-11-25) is read as unavailable, never as a partial list, until #188. Visit and reminder tests use the values the readers return (`tests/ntuh_reads.py`), not pages. Markup that does not produce a complete card fails closed as `schema_drift`. Accuracy against the room's physical indicator is unproven.

The same evidence rules out two things the reader must not start doing:

- **A listed card does not mean the clinic is consulting.** Cards appear as soon as a session is scheduled for the day, hours before it starts, and stay listed after the clinic has finished. Card presence says the clinic is scheduled for the requested session, nothing more, which is why the watch is keyed on the number rather than on the card.
- **The current light is the number most recently called, not a queue position.** It can jump to any checked-in number: a 44-number backward move was observed within the same session, when a doctor called a number that had been waiting since the start. Nothing may read it as "how far the clinic has got".

The start and end signals come from two clinics watched through their transition on 2026-10-29: both kept their card and blanked their number when they finished, while a clinic that had not started showed a blank number and a blank highest-called number on its detail page. That is one department on one morning. What it does not cover: whether a clinic can blank its number temporarily and resume, what a cancelled clinic looks like, and what happens in a room when its next session begins. The two-blank-reads rule is the margin left for the first of those.

### The per-clinic detail page (observed 2026-11-20, read on the visit day since #163)

The list card links to `ClinicCurrentLightNoDetail?ServiceIDSE=<id>`, a public page per clinic. Reads of seven clinics in 眼科 and 內科 on 2026-11-20 (`research/private/source-evidence/ntuh-per-doctor-2026-11-20.md`, raw pages under `raw/ntuh-2026-11-20*/`) showed:

- It shows the current light with a flag (過號, 檢查號, 報到順序NN), 已叫最大號, 預計叫號 with a type ((順號), (過號), (報到NN), (無)) or as one bare number with no type (outside 眼科, already on a 2026-11-20 page; read as an expected call of unknown kind since #243), and the status of every number not yet finished: 未報到, 已報到 (報到N in check-in-order clinics), 初診 or 看診中. Its legend says a number below the highest called has been passed, which holds only where 已叫最大號 is the last number called (眼科; see "Observed across five departments" below).
- A substitute doctor shows as `總醫師<span id='subname_txt'>(代)</span>` inside `doc-name` (2026-11-25). The detail page's doctor is read with its `(代)`, as on the list card; it is not compared or shown.
- The card's current light was not the queue front in any clinic read: 09診 showed 022 with 已叫最大號 080, and a 內科 clinic's card went 022, 042, 008 within an hour while its highest called stayed at 025.
- Finished numbers drop off the list, but checked-in numbers also left before
  their turn (敬老號, cancellations, transfers: 22 on 2026-11-20) and none came
  back. A page is not cleared after its session: leftover numbers and a frozen
  highest called stayed until 17:25. No page says 已看診, 完診, 停診 or 休診,
  and none shows a start time or session.
- The page shows department, room and doctor, but not the date, session or clinic type, so identity comes only from the list card that links to it.
- `ServiceIDSE` is one per card per day: the same ID all day from 00:40, two IDs for one doctor with two cards, and absent from the clinic schedule pages. It is never stored beyond its day.
- The department list has no sub-specialty: a 胃腸肝膽 clinic is just a 內科 card.

ADR 0003 and the #136 grilling decide how reminders will use this page. The reader now reads it (Reading rules, "Detail page"); reminder rules still use only the current light until the rest of #136 lands.

### Observed across five departments (2026-11-25)

Live provider, read from a home connection, not from the Worker: selector, list, detail and clinic schedule pages of 內科 `MED`, 骨科 `ORTH`, 眼科 `OPH`, 外科部 `SURG` and 神經部 `NEUR`, at 10:15 (morning) and 12:45, 13:50 and 15:00 (afternoon). The evidence note is private; the committed copies and their replay tests are `tests/fixtures/hospitals/ntuh_captures/`, `tests/test_ntuh_capture_replay.py`, `tests/test_ntuh_afternoon_replay.py` and `tests/test_ntuh_schedule_replay.py` (#205, #243). These are page facts. The reader still serves only 眼科, and the rules above are unchanged by them.

- **Access.** 87 requests in bursts of about one every 2.2 seconds, all 200 with no challenge. That is not a rate-limit test, and Worker egress is still unverified.
- **List card and detail page can disagree within seconds.** 30 of 32 card/detail pairs matched; 內科 02診 showed 002 on the card and 003 順號 on the detail page 12 seconds later, and 骨科 05診 showed 023 on the card and 014 過號 17 seconds later. Only the detail page carries the flag: 神經部 05診 showed 005 on the card and 005 過號 on the detail page. Seven afternoon cards in 內科, 骨科 and 外科部 went down between 13:50 and 15:00 (for example 059 to 019); none had its detail page captured, so the cause is open.
- **已叫最大號 means different things by department.** In 眼科 it is the last number called and equals the light on every 順號 read. In 內科, 骨科, 外科部 and 神經部 it is the last entry of 預計叫號, which can be a number that has not checked in and so has not been called. Over the pages captured on 2026-11-20, 11-23 and 11-25 outside 眼科 and 皮膚部 (check-in-order pages and blank values left out), the labelled number was itself still listed as waiting on 95 of 101 pages, and was the last 預計叫號 entry on 80 (recount of 2026-11-26, #180). So "a number below 已叫最大號 has been passed", which the page's own legend says, is true only in 眼科. 已叫最大號 also holds still for 敬老號 and 檢查號 calls, and is blank in check-in-order clinics. The reader therefore reports it as highest called only when the page proves it (#248, ADR 0003).
- **預計叫號.** Outside 眼科 its first entry is the light itself (15 of 18 typed lists); in 眼科 it never is (0 of 12). A bare number with no type appears outside 眼科 only, and `000 (無)` in 眼科 only.
- **`------`** in place of the light and 預計叫號 means only "no light now": before the first call in a number-order clinic, with an empty roster, and after a number. It does not mark check-in order or the clinic's end.
- **A check-in-order card shows a number.** 骨科 07診: card 019, detail page 019 報到順序27 with 19 看診中. The light is the patient number being seen, not a check-in position.
- **Before the session opens, the clinic's page already exists**, with a blank light, blank 已叫最大號 and 預計叫號, and its full roster (眼科 clinics already had 12 to 16 已報到 at 12:45). No afternoon card had a light at 12:45; at 13:50 most did, and 眼科 was the slowest (3 of 15, 7 of 15 at 15:00). Afternoon cards carry their own `ServiceIDSE`, so a morning page never stands in for an afternoon one.
- **Card clinic types** across 3,242 captured cards are only 普通門診, 醫師約診, 教學門診, 一日手術 (眼科, 骨科, 外科部) and 檢查門診 (眼科), or blank.
- **Clinic schedule pages.** Measured in decoded characters with CRLF kept: 內科 7,684,379 (485 clinics) and 外科部 3,949,248 (251) are both over the reader's 3.5 million cap; 眼科 2,208,725 (136), 骨科 2,037,215 (126) and 神經部 1,846,246 (115) fit. Size grows about 15.8 K characters per clinic, so the cap fits about 215 clinics. A page covers today through today plus 14, weekdays only, which is 10 or 11 day blocks depending on the weekday it is read (11 on 2026-11-25). Each page carries 3 token values although the GET needs none, and its Incapsula script is hex-encoded, so searching the page for `incap` finds nothing. Every legend, back to 2026-11-03, also lists 到診不續掛.
- **Schedule host.** Every earlier capture came from `reg.ntuh.gov.tw`; the reader requests `registration.ntuh.gov.tw`. One GET of the reader's exact 眼科 URL at 15:44 on 2026-11-25 returned the full page (200, 136 clinics), so both hosts serve it.

## Future-date schedule reader (Issue #58)

The today-only `DeptLightTable` path above covers the live queue number. A separate reader, enabled by the same `NTUH_READER_ENABLED` flag, serves `list_clinics` for dates in the next 14 days against the dept-wide `RegDeptSchedule` page.

The pre-flight path answers one question only: for the chosen visit date, department and (session, room, doctor), is this clinic published on NTUH's own schedule? It does not read queue numbers, and the queue-side `DeptLightTable` reader keeps its today-only contract unchanged.

### How this read is made

One GET against `GET https://registration.ntuh.gov.tw/WebReg/WebReg/RegDeptSchedule?vhospCode=T0&showBlock=A&vDeptCode=<DeptCode>&realSubDeptCode=showAll`, with `Accept-Language: zh-TW` and `Localization.CurrentUICulture=zh-TW`. The same page is also served on `reg.ntuh.gov.tw`, where the captures came from. No token is sent (the page carries 3 token values, but the GET needs none), no POST. The returned page lists every weekday from today through today plus 14 (10 or 11 day blocks) with its sessions and per-doctor clinic cards. The reader refuses a page over 3.5 million characters as unavailable, which on 2026-11-25 meant 內科 and 外科部, until #188. The parser pins the day-column `MM/DD` against the caller's Taipei today year, so a window that crosses December into January still resolves correctly.

Per clinic card the reader records `(visit_date, session, room, doctor, 診別, status)` from the schedule's own markup; the modal's 預約碼 is observed as evidence (`research/private/source-evidence/ntuh-schedule-2026-11-03.md`) but is not stored, since the visit-day card does not carry it.

### Match contract

Pre-flight matches on `(visit_date, session, room, doctor)` only. The schedule's `診別` vocabulary (`角膜`, `網膜`, `教學門診`, `葡萄膜炎`, `眼神經`, `眼整型`, …) is different from the visit-day card's `clinic_type` vocabulary (`普通門診`, `醫師約診`, `教學門診`, `一日手術`, `檢查門診`, or blank, across 3,242 cards captured up to 2026-11-25), and the two sets overlap only on `教學門診`. The schedule's 診別 stays on the visit's stored `clinic_code` (for example `03/醫師/角膜`) until a same-day Start resolves it. A visit started or read on its day is matched to the day's card by the identification in Reading rules; a same-day Start that finds the card by room and doctor updates the stored code to that card's code, while one that finds only a substitute card keeps the booked doctor's code.

### Status markers

Cards carry a status marker inside `<div class="tag"><div class="first-reg">...</div></div>`. The reader drops rows whose status is `停診` or `颱風停診` (the clinic will not run that day). Registration-restriction markers (`額滿`, `已逾當日掛號時間`, `代診不續掛`, `代診可續掛`, `僅存本院初診`, `本院初診`, `本科初診`, `本科複診`, `18:00開放`, and `到診不續掛`, which every captured legend also lists) leave the clinic running and keep the row in the roster, since a patient who already has a number is unaffected by registration-side limits.

Each listed clinic also carries a dropdown `label` (`room · doctor（session）`, for example `05 · 合成醫師乙（早診）`) and ordered `details`. Today's roster lists 診間, 醫師, 診別 and 時段 from the light-table card. A future day lists 診間, 醫師, 科別 (the text before the `|` in `.sub-spec`), 診別, 時段 and, when the card shows one, 狀態 (the status marker verbatim, which is where 額滿 and 代診 markers appear).

### Reason codes returned to the setup picker and Start

- `visit_date_past` - the chosen date is before Taipei today. Returned by both `list_clinics` and Start.
- `visit_date_out_of_schedule_window` - the chosen date is more than 14 days ahead, so the clinic schedule does not cover it yet. The reader answers without contacting NTUH and adds `schedule_end` (Taipei today plus 14 days) and `release_time` (00:00 Taipei, 14 days before the chosen date, per NTUH's "Schedule from midnight on 14 days before consultation"). The setup form shows the not-yet-published notice from these and degrades to manual entry.
- `scope_mismatch` - the hospital or department is outside the supported scope (not NTUH main or not OPH).
- `no_observation`, `access_denied`, `timeout`, `schema_drift` - transport or shape failures from the schedule page itself; the picker degrades to manual entry and Start fails closed as `schedule_unavailable`.
- `doctor_not_scheduled` - the schedule read succeeded, but no row for the chosen date matches on `(session, room, doctor)`. Only Start returns this; the picker catches it earlier by refusing a dropdown mismatch.

### Daily sweep (Issue #58, R1)

A once-per-Taipei-day sweep runs over every draft whose `visit_date` is after today and that has not been checked today, for hospitals that publish a clinic schedule. No day count bounds the query; the reader decides whether a day is published. Drafts of the same `(hospital, department)` share one schedule fetch. For each draft the sweep matches on `(session, room, doctor)`; a draft whose doctor is no longer on the schedule, or whose matching row has status `停診`/`颱風停診`, transitions to `scope_mismatch` and raises the standard organizer-attention signal. A schedule read that fails is a no-op this run; the sweep retries the next day. Drafts whose `visit_date` is today are handled by the Start-time resolve step, not the sweep. A day the hospital has not published yet marks the draft checked today and leaves its source-read status untouched, so the next Taipei day asks again; the reader answers that without contacting the hospital.

## Recovery

- Unavailable with a timeout, no observation or no result: wait; the reader retries by itself while the clinic is still calling. Start again after the reason clears.
- `card_not_found` on the visit day: NTUH's list of the day's cards has no card for the visit's session, room and doctor. Open the official page, pick the same department and session, and compare the card's room and doctor against the visit; a clinic that was cancelled or moved is the usual cause. The reader keeps looking and the organizer decides.
- `substitute_doctor`: the card in the visit's room names a substitute doctor (`住院醫師(代)`, `主治醫師(代)`). Reminders continue; the organizer decides whether the family still goes.
- `ambiguous_match`: several of the day's cards fit (for example one doctor with two clinic types in one room and session). Nothing is sent from a guess; pick the clinic again.
- A clinic that has not called a number yet is not a failure: Start is accepted once the card is identified, and the visit runs once a number appears; after a Start before the day's scheduled reads begin, the next read is the first of them. Start is also accepted on a clinic whose page went blank after it called, which may be a pause or the end, and the visit holds the one running place until it is stopped or the day ends.
- Access refused: do not retry by hand around it. Open the official page in a browser to see what changed. The block lasts for the rest of that day, for that department and session. #179 widens it.
- Scope mismatch or schema drift: correct the department and clinic code, or update the parser after a fresh manual check of the page.
- `visit_date_out_of_schedule_window` on a draft: the chosen date is after the clinic schedule end date, so the hospital has not published that day yet. The draft is held; the daily sweep will validate it on the first sweep after the hospital publishes that day.
- `doctor_not_scheduled` or `schedule_unavailable` at Start: open the setup form, re-pick the doctor from the dropdown (now constrained to the schedule), or wait a few minutes and retry if the source was briefly down.
